Questions

Do I need to pay for Notion?

No. Everything works on Notion's free personal plan.

Does it really work on a phone?

Yes — that was a design constraint, not an afterthought. Every table shows at most three columns with the title frozen in place, and the boards and calendars are built for a small screen.

My university doesn't use a 4.0 GPA scale.

Open the Courses database and edit the Grade Points formula to match your scale. Everything downstream recalculates automatically.

How do I get it after paying?

You get the Notion template link immediately after checkout. Click Duplicate and the whole system — databases, formulas and connections — copies into your own workspace.

Can I delete parts I don't need?

Yes. No job? Delete the Jobs Area. The only page to leave alone is System Databases, which every view reads from.

Is it in Arabic?

The template itself is in English, which is what most universities teach in. This page is bilingual, and you can rename any page or field in Notion to Arabic in seconds.
